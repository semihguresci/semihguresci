Legal

Data policy

What data may be processed, why it is used, and how long it may be retained.

Purpose of processing

Data related to this website is used to operate, secure, and deliver the site and to respond to professional inquiries. If you opt into analytics, I use it to understand which pages people visit and how they find and use the site.

Data sources

Data may come from standard server logs, email communication, interactions on external platforms linked from this website, and Google Analytics when you opt in. This site does not maintain an account database or customer relationship system.

Retention

Technical log data is kept only as long as the hosting environment requires. Emails and professional messages may be kept as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain ongoing communication, or keep a record of relevant professional correspondence.

Analytics cookies last up to 180 days. Google Analytics retains user-level and event-level data according to the retention settings for this site's property.

Sharing

Personal data is not sold or shared for advertising purposes. Infrastructure or platform providers may process data as part of hosting, email delivery, source-code hosting, or professional networking services. Google processes analytics data when you opt in.

Your choices

You can reject analytics or withdraw your consent at any time through Analytics preferences in the footer. You can browse the site without an account.

Updates

This policy may be updated if the website's functionality, tools, or data handling changes.
