Legal

Data policy

What data may be processed, why it is used, and how long it may be retained.

Purpose of processing

Any data related to this website is used only to operate, secure, and deliver the site, and for direct professional communication started by visitors.

Data sources

Data may come from standard server logs, email communication, and interactions on external platforms linked from this website. This site does not maintain an account database or customer relationship system.

Retention

Technical log data is kept only as long as the hosting environment requires. Emails and professional messages may be kept as needed to respond to inquiries, maintain ongoing communication, or keep a record of relevant professional correspondence.

Sharing

Personal data is not sold or shared for advertising purposes. Infrastructure or platform providers may process data strictly as part of hosting, email delivery, source-code hosting, or professional networking services.

Your choices

You can choose not to contact me by email or through third-party platforms. The site works without accounts, and you can use it without sending any information to me.

Updates

This policy may be updated if the website's functionality, tools, or data handling changes.
