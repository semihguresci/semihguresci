Privacy policy

How this website handles visitor information and communication data.

This website is a personal portfolio and blog operated by Semih Güreşçi. It provides information and does not require account registration, logins, or direct form submissions.

Information collected

This website does not intentionally collect personal data through on-site forms or user accounts. Information you send me by email or through third-party platforms such as LinkedIn or GitHub is shared voluntarily.

Technical data

Hosting providers and platform services may process standard technical data in server logs for security, reliability, and traffic delivery. This data may include IP addresses, browser type, device information, and access times.

Cookies, storage, and tracking

This website does not use advertising trackers, analytics, or profiling cookies, and it does not write preferences to browser storage. The interactive graphics demo runs on your device and does not send data to a server.

This website links to third-party services including GitHub and LinkedIn. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
